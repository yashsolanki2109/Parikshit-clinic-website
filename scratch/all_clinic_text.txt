Department One
Dental Treatments
Preventive and periodontal care, root canal and pulp treatments, orthodontics, restorative treatments, dental implants, and cosmetic dental procedures.
Preventive &amp; General Care
Routine Exams
Clinical dental evaluations and digital X-rays when indicated.
Your dentist will review your concerns and relevant dental history, examine your mouth, and explain findings. X-rays are considered only when clinically needed.
Teeth Cleanings
Professional removal of debris, calculus, and surface stains, followed by polishing of the teeth.
The visit can include a gum-health review and home-care advice. Your clinician will explain what is recommended for your needs.
Dental Fillings
Tooth-coloured composite restorations.
After examining the tooth, your dentist can explain suitable filling materials, the steps involved, and expected aftercare.
Endodontic &amp; Pulp Therapy
Root Canal Treatment
Treatment for infection or damage involving the pulp inside a tooth.
The dentist will assess the tooth, discuss alternatives and explain the treatment stages. Several visits or a final restoration may be needed, depending on the case.
Pulpectomy
Pulp treatment specially designed for selected deciduous (primary/baby) teeth.
The dentist will assess whether the tooth can be restored, discuss suitable alternatives, and explain the expected follow-up.
Advanced Periodontal Therapy
Flap Surgery
Periodontal pocket reduction and access for treatment of affected tissues.
A periodontal assessment determines whether this approach is appropriate; the clinician discusses alternatives, recovery, and follow-up.
Gingivectomy
Surgical reshaping or removal of excess gum tissue when indicated.
A consultation reviews gum health and the reason for reshaping; the clinician explains the proposed approach and healing.
Frenectomy
Release of restricted muscle attachments when clinically appropriate.
Assessment considers the attachment and related concerns; the clinician explains whether release or another approach may be appropriate.
Local Drug Delivery
Targeted antimicrobial application in selected periodontal pockets.
This may be discussed as part of a wider periodontal plan; the clinician explains the medicine, application, and follow-up.
Non-surgical Periodontal Therapy
Scaling and root planing to manage periodontal disease.
The plan depends on gum measurements and findings; your clinician reviews home-care steps and maintenance visits.
Regenerative &amp; Reconstructive Surgery
Regenerative Surgery
Selected regenerative techniques and bone grafting to support tissue repair.
A specialist evaluates the site and explains whether regenerative methods may be considered, along with alternatives and recovery.
Ostectomy
Surgical bone contouring to reshape supporting structures when indicated.
Your clinician discusses the reason for bone contouring, nearby structures, the planned steps, and follow-up.
Ridge Augmentation
Procedures to restore jawbone width or height in suitable cases.
Assessment considers jawbone shape and future restoration goals; options, healing time, and alternatives are reviewed.
Plastic &amp; Cosmetic Dentistry
Periodontal Plastic Surgery
Gum procedures to address selected functional or aesthetic concerns.
Consultation explores gum health, functional or aesthetic goals, available approaches, and expected healing.
Smile Makeovers
Individualised smile assessment and aesthetic treatment planning.
Planning starts with your goals and an oral-health assessment; the dentist explains possible sequences, options, and limitations.
Porcelain Veneers
Custom-made shells to improve the appearance of suitable teeth.
Your dentist reviews tooth condition and bite, discusses alternatives and preparation, and explains care and maintenance.
Teeth Whitening
Professional whitening options following a suitability assessment.
Assessment checks tooth and gum health and existing restorations; your dentist discusses suitable options and possible sensitivity.
Implantology &amp; Restorative Care
Dental Implants
Implant-based tooth replacement following clinical assessment.
Consultation reviews oral health and bone, any imaging needed, alternatives, stages, timing, risks, and maintenance.
Crowns &amp; Bridges
Restorations using suitable ceramic, zirconia or other materials.
The dentist evaluates the teeth and bite, then discusses restoration materials, preparation, expected visits, and care.
Mouth Reconstruction
Comprehensive restorative planning, including implant-supported rehabilitation when appropriate.
A staged plan may combine services based on examination findings; the dentist explains sequence, alternatives, timelines, and maintenance.
Specialized Dental Care
Pediatric Dentistry
Dental assessment and care tailored to children's needs.
The visit is tailored to a child&#39;s age and needs; the team can explain the planned assessment, preventive advice, and next steps.
Emergency Dentistry
Assessment of urgent dental pain, injury and other dental concerns.
Call the clinic to discuss urgent symptoms and current availability; an examination helps determine next steps and whether referral is needed.
Orthodontic Treatment
Assessment and planning for tooth alignment and bite concerns.
Options may include braces or other clinician-guided appliances. The approach, timing, and follow-up depend on an orthodontic assessment.
Department Two
Facial Aesthetics &amp; Cosmetic Treatments
Skin, facial rejuvenation, hair restoration and cosmetic services. Treatment suitability depends on assessment and applicable professional requirements.
Mole Removal
Assessment of the lesion and appropriate options where indicated.
Consultation reviews the area and whether assessment by another specialist is needed; suitable options and healing are discussed.
Freckle Removal
Assessment of pigmentation and suitable cosmetic treatment options.
Consultation considers pigmentation, skin type, and medical history; available options, aftercare, and variable results are discussed.
Eyelid Xanthelasma Removal
Assessment of cholesterol deposits around the eyelids and appropriate treatment or referral.
The clinician assesses the eyelid area and suitability, and may recommend medical assessment or referral before cosmetic treatment.
Ozone High-Frequency Facial
Facial treatment incorporating high-frequency equipment and an LED mask, according to the protocol offered.
The clinic can explain its exact protocol, equipment, suitability screening, risks, and aftercare during consultation.
Plasma Skin Tightening
Skin-tightening treatment assessment and discussion of suitable options.
Consultation covers the device and technique used, skin suitability, risks, aftercare, and other available options.
Chemical Peel
Skin exfoliation treatment selected according to skin type and concerns.
Selection considers skin type, concern, and history; the clinician explains preparation, aftercare, recovery, and possible reactions.
HydraFacial Treatment
Facial cleansing, exfoliation and hydration according to the protocol offered.
The clinic can explain the products and steps included, suitability, possible reactions, and aftercare for your skin.
Vampire Facial (Microneedling)
Microneedling-based facial treatment; confirm the exact protocol offered at the clinic.
Consultation explains whether microneedling or PRP is included, the exact protocol, suitability, risks, recovery, and aftercare.
Face PRP Treatment
Facial platelet-rich plasma treatment following a suitability assessment.
The practitioner explains the preparation and application process, suitability, alternatives, risks, and aftercare.
Hair PRP Treatment
Scalp PRP treatment assessment for selected hair-related concerns.
Consultation reviews scalp concerns and alternatives, and explains the protocol, follow-up, expected course, and limits.
Permanent Hair Reduction
Hair reduction treatment using the technology available at the clinic. Results vary by individual.
The clinician discusses the device used, skin and hair characteristics, preparation, likely treatment schedule, and variable results.
Permanent Eyebrow Microblading
Cosmetic eyebrow enhancement using a microblading technique.
Consultation covers brow design, pigment, hygiene, healing, aftercare, touch-up planning, and suitability.
Treatment availability, eligibility, risks and expected outcomes should be discussed during consultation. Please confirm that each advertised procedure is within your qualifications, applicable local regulations and actual clinic offerings.

Patient Information
Frequently Asked Questions
Helpful information before your visit.
How do I book an appointment?
Choose a preferred date and time. The clinic will contact you to confirm availability.
How do I know which treatment is suitable for me?
Suitability depends on individual assessment. Your practitioner can discuss potential benefits, risks and alternatives.
Can I ask about both dental and facial aesthetic services?
Yes. Choose the relevant department in the appointment form, or send a separate request for each service.
Are treatment results guaranteed?
No procedure can guarantee a particular outcome. Results and recovery vary depending on the treatment and individual circumstances.
What happens at my first appointment?
We will ask about your concerns and relevant history, examine as needed, and discuss any findings and options. Any treatment plan is individual.
Should I bring anything to my visit?
If available, bring relevant previous dental records or imaging and a current list of medicines and allergies. Call the clinic if you are unsure what is needed.
How long will my appointment take?
Visit length depends on the reason for your appointment and whether assessment or treatment is planned. Contact the clinic to confirm an expected duration.
Can I walk in without an appointment?
Please call or WhatsApp first to check current availability, especially for urgent concerns.
How can I change or cancel an appointment?
Contact the clinic by phone or WhatsApp as early as possible. The team can explain the current rescheduling policy.
How much will my treatment cost?
Fees depend on your assessment and the treatment plan. Ask the clinic for an estimate and discuss options before proceeding.
Do you provide dental check-ups for children?
Yes. Pediatric dental assessments and care are listed among the clinic's services. Contact the clinic to confirm age suitability, appointment availability, and any preparation needed.
What happens during a facial aesthetics consultation?
You can discuss your goals and relevant health history. The practitioner should explain suitable options, potential risks, alternatives, and aftercare before you decide.
How should I prepare for a treatment?
Preparation varies by treatment. Follow the instructions provided by your clinician and ask them about medicines or health conditions; do not change prescribed medicines without professional guidance.
What should I do if I have an urgent dental concern?
Call the clinic to describe the concern and check availability. For a serious medical emergency, seek urgent local medical care.
